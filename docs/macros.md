# Macro reference

Generated by `tools/gen_macro_docs.py` from `src/framework_bringup/param/macros.yaml`; do not edit by hand.
38 macros, 38 on the whitelist (the only ones the LLM/SLM may call; macros may call any macro).

A task spec calls one as `{"macro": "<name>", "args": {...}}`. `arg?` = optional (has a default).

| Macro | Args | What it does | LLM may call |
|---|---|---|---|
| `acknowledge_person` | - | Find a person, turn to face them and say hello (a greeting only: it does not follow or move with them). | yes |

| `receive_guest` | slot? | Wait for the doorbell, go to the door, ask the guest's name and favourite drink and remember them under a slot (opening the door is NOT automated: a person must open it). Example: `{"slot": "guest1"}`. | yes |
